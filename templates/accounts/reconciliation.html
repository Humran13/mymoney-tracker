Opening balance reconciliation

A read-only review. Nothing here changes your money — the only action available is setting an account's opening-balance date, and only after you confirm it.

How an opening balance works here

Your opening balance is the money actually in the account at the start of the snapshot date. Everything dated on or after that date is added on top; anything dated before it is kept as historical record but does not change today's balance — it is already inside the opening balance.
